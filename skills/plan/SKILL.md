# Plan

## Plan the work

Define done with a check that can fail. Estimate the work and identify blockers. Split it into independently verifiable units and tackle the riskiest unknown first. Show the phase list before a long run. Capture a baseline, parallelize only independent work, verify each unit on the real artifact and check the whole at the end. Use **decision-log** for significant choices and results.

## Sketch the design

Trace callers, storage and interfaces. For hard-to-reverse choices, sketch two designs with types, signatures and module boundaries. Prefer clear ownership of state, one obvious path per task and fewer details for callers to know. Record the chosen shape in the ticket. Revisit it if workarounds keep appearing.

## Sharpen the brief

Write the goal, acceptance, verification and scope. Consult the raw evidence and name the method and observable proof that fit the task. Keep the original scope. Test choices that evidence can settle and leave product, taste and cost decisions to the owner.
